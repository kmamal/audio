The head-related impulse responses in resources/hrtf-ircam-composite.s16le are
the composite subject (IRC_Composite_C_R0195) assembled by the WebKit project
from the LISTEN HRTF database, measured at IRCAM and AKG in 2002-2003:

  http://recherche.ircam.fr/equipes/salles/listen/

IRCAM distributes the LISTEN database freely for any use and asks that it be
acknowledged in any publication or development that results from its use. The
composite was obtained from the Chromium source tree
(third_party/blink/renderer/platform/audio/resources/Composite.flac) and
converted to raw 16-bit little-endian PCM without further processing. The file
holds 240 stereo responses of 256 frames at 44100 Hz: for each azimuth from 0 to
345 degrees in 15 degree steps, the elevations 0, 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, -45,
-30 and -15 degrees, in that order.
